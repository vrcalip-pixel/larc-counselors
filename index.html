The reframe

It’s not jobs. It’s tasks.

AI is changing tasks faster than it’s eliminating jobs. Help students see the tasks, build the skills, and earn the evidence.

Instead of

“Will my career be replaced by AI?”

Ask

“Which tasks in this career are changing, and which still need human judgment?”

The toolkit

Six questions for any advising conversation.

Use these in any advising session, with any student, in any field.

  1. 01 How might AI show up in the career field you’re considering?
  2. 02 What tasks in that work might be supported by AI? Which still need human judgment?
  3. 03 What human skills will still matter — communication, ethics, judgment, creativity?
  4. 04 What’s one piece of evidence — a project, certificate, or course — you could build this year?
  5. 05 What’s one dual-enrollment, CTE, or college course that could move you forward right now?
  6. 06 How can you use AI responsibly while still doing your own thinking?

What students need

The skill stack that wins.

01
AI literacy Prompt literacy. Output evaluation. Ethics in AI. Not coding.
02
Human skills Communication. Judgment. Collaboration. Ethics. Empathy.
03
Domain knowledge The actual field they want to enter — health, business, public service, the trades.
04
Evidence of capability Projects. Certificates. Internships. Dual-enrollment artifacts.

Pathway types

Categories every counselor should know.

  • Dual enrollment + CCAP partnerships
  • College Promise programs (varies by district)
  • Strong Workforce CTE pathways
  • Industry-aligned certificates
  • Internships and work-based learning

What the data shows

Statewide outcomes that hold up.

  • 165,000 California dual enrollees; LA/OC = 29% of state
  • Strong Workforce: 74% job placement, 47% living wage
  • 2.2M students across the CCC system
  • BLS: data scientists +34%, cybersecurity +29% (2024–34)
